# Marketing & Growth Skills Suite

A production-grade collection of marketing, SEO, conversion, and copywriting workflows curated for agentic development.

## Core SEO & Visibility Skills

- `ai-seo`: Optimize content to be cited by AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini). Generates `llms.txt`, citation targets, and schema structures.
- `programmatic-seo`: Architect and build data-driven programmatic landing pages at scale with quality safeguards.
- `seo-audit`: Full technical and on-page SEO diagnostics (crawling, indexing, Core Web Vitals, metadata, search intent).
- `schema`: Design and implement rich JSON-LD structured data for products, articles, FAQs, organizations, and knowledge graphs.
- `site-architecture`: Design crawlable, user-friendly topic clusters, internal linking, and taxonomy trees.

## Content & Copywriting Skills

- `copywriting`: Write compelling marketing copy using proven frameworks (PAS, AIDA, Before-After-Bridge).
- `copy-editing`: Polish and strengthen prose for clarity, punch, rhythm, and conversion impact.
- `content-strategy`: Develop customer-journey mapped content roadmaps that drive organic acquisition.

## Conversion & Growth

- `cro`: Conversion rate optimization audit and experiments for landing pages and funnels.
- `ab-testing`: Statistical experiment design, sample sizing, and hypothesis validation.
- `pricing`: Value-based pricing strategy, packaging tiers, and price-point testing.
- `onboarding`: User activation flows, time-to-value acceleration, and product tours.
- `cold-email`: B2B outbound campaign strategy, personalization tokens, and deliverability checks.
- `marketing-psychology`: Apply cognitive biases, mental models, and persuasion principles ethically.
